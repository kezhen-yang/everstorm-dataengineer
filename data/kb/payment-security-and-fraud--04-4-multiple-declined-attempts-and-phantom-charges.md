## 4. Multiple declined attempts and phantom charges

Each attempt may leave a **pending authorisation** on your account. These are
holds, not charges. No money has moved.

They drop off automatically in 3–5 business days, or up to 8 on some
international issuers. You can ask your bank to release them sooner; we cannot,
because a declined authorisation never reached us in a form we can act on.

If you see several identical pending lines after a failed checkout, this is what
they are. They will not become charges.
