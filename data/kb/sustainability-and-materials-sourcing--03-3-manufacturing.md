## 3. Manufacturing

| Product family | Country | Facility type |
|---|---|---|
| Merino base layers | New Zealand | Owned |
| Shells and softshells | Vietnam | Long-term partner, 9 years |
| Footwear | Portugal | Long-term partner, 12 years |
| Packs | Vietnam | Long-term partner |
| Electronics | China | Contract manufacturer |

All facilities are audited annually against the Fair Labor Association code.
Audit summaries are published at everstorm.example/responsibility.

We do not own most of our manufacturing, and we are not going to claim the level
of control that ownership would give us.
