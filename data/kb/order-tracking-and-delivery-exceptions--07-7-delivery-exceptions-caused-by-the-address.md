## 7. Delivery exceptions caused by the address

The most common are an incomplete apartment number, a business address with no
suite, or a name mismatch at a secure building. Carriers will usually hold the
parcel for 3–5 business days and attempt redelivery twice.

If you get an exception notice, respond to the carrier directly — they can act
on your instruction immediately, whereas a request routed through us adds a day.
After the hold period the parcel returns to the fulfilment centre and we refund
it automatically.
