# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Everstorm Outfitters — Revision 4.0, effective 18 May 2025**

This revision replaces Revision 3.0 in full. Where an older printed or cached
copy disagrees with this document, this document governs. The most visible
changes from 3.0 are the free-shipping threshold dropping from $99 to $75, the
addition of Japan and Singapore as served destinations, and the move to
duties-paid (DDP) delivery for the EU.
