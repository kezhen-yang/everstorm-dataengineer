## 3. Store credit

We issue store credit in four situations:

1. **Gift returns.** Refunded as credit to the recipient, and the original
   purchaser is not notified. See `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md` §10.
2. **Lifetime Repair Credit.** Credit equal to a quoted repair cost if you would
   rather buy new. See `renew-workshop-repair-program.md` §3.
3. **Goodwill.** Service failures, delays, and the occasional apology.
4. **Where a refund cannot reach you** — a closed card account, or a crypto
   refund you would rather not take at the day's rate.

Store credit arrives as a code by email and is applied at checkout like a gift
card. It is tied to the email address it was issued to and cannot be
transferred.
