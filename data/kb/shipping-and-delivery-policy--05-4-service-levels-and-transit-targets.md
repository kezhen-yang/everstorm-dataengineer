## 4. Service levels and transit targets

Transit targets are in **business days after dispatch**, and they are targets,
not guarantees.

| Region | Standard | Expedited | Carrier |
|---|---|---|---|
| US West Coast | 2–3 | 1 | UPS Ground / UPS Air Saver |
| US Midwest & Texas | 3–4 | 2 | UPS Ground / UPS 2-Day Air |
| US East Coast | 4–5 | 2 | UPS Ground / UPS Air Saver |
| Canada | 5–8 | 3 | Canada Post / DHL Express |
| EU & UK | 5–8 | 3 | DPD / DHL Express |
| Australia & New Zealand | 7–10 | 4 | FedEx International Priority |
| Japan & Singapore | 4–6 | 2 | DHL Express |

Expedited service carries a delivery commitment. If an expedited shipment
misses its committed date by more than 24 hours for a reason within our or the
carrier's control, we refund the expedited surcharge — see §9.
