## 8. Defect and warranty claims

Manufacturing defects — seam failure, sole separation, zipper loss — are
covered for **12 months**. Choose **Warranty Claim** in the portal and upload
three photos showing the defect. Approved claims ship a replacement the next
business day.

Full criteria, including what counts as a defect versus wear, are in
`warranty-and-defect-claims.md`.
