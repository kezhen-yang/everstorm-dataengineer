## 6. Breaking in

The Summit GTX needs 15–25 miles of easy walking before a big trip. Wear it for
errands, then short walks, then a loaded day hike.

The Creek Approach Shoe needs essentially no break-in.

Break-in softens the flex and moulds the footbed. **It does not make a boot
bigger.** If it is the wrong size, more miles will not fix it, and you will have
worn the boot past the point where it can be returned.
