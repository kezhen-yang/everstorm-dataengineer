## M-110 · Peak season EU/UK delay

> You're right that it's running late, and I want to be straight with you about
> why. EU and UK parcels move through a DHL hub that runs at capacity through
> December, and DHL suspends its service guarantees for the period.
>
> That means our usual 5–8 business days is realistically 8–16 right now. I
> can't give you a firm date and I'd rather not invent one.
>
> {IF_EXPEDITED} You paid for expedited, so I'm refunding that surcharge now.

**Agent notes.** Always refund the expedited surcharge in peak without making
them ask. We cannot refund standard shipping for weather or peak delay, because
standard carries a target rather than a commitment.
