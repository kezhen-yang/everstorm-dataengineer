## 4. Canadian duties and taxes

Canadian orders ship **DDU**. You paid GST/HST, any provincial tax, and a
Canada Post brokerage fee at delivery.

None of that is refundable by us, because we never received it. The Canada
Border Services Agency operates a formal refund process for returned goods
(Form B2G, Informal Adjustment Request). We will supply the commercial invoice
and proof of export on request — email claims@everstorm.example.

Realistically, for an order under about CAD 150 the reclaim paperwork costs
more time than it returns in money. We say so rather than let you find out
afterwards.
