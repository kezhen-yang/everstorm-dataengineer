## 3. Processing times

Orders placed Monday to Friday before 14:00 local warehouse time ship the same
day. Orders placed after that cut-off ship the next business day.

Weekend orders ship on Monday, except where Monday is a public holiday at the
serving warehouse, in which case they ship the next working day. Rotterdam
observes Dutch public holidays; Reno and Harrisburg observe US federal
holidays.

Processing time is separate from transit time. A West Coast order placed at
13:00 on a Friday ships that Friday and is quoted 2–3 business days of transit
on top.
