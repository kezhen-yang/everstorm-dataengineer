## 7. Canvas packs

Remove the frame sheet before doing anything. Hand-wipe with mild soap and a
damp cloth, then air dry completely.

Never machine wash. It strips the wax, deforms the pack, and can delaminate
foam in the back panel.

Re-wax annually, or whenever water soaks in rather than beading. Warm the wax
slightly, work it in with a cloth, then heat the surface gently with a hairdryer
to draw it into the weave.
