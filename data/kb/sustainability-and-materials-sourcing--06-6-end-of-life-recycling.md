## 6. End-of-life recycling

Send anything back with a free recycling label:

| Material | Destination |
|---|---|
| Merino | Reprocessed into insulation batting |
| Synthetic shell fabric | Mechanically recycled into industrial felt |
| Footwear | Ground for track and playground surfacing |
| Down | Cleaned and re-lofted into new products |
| Electronics and lithium cells | Certified e-waste processing |

Electronics are processed domestically. We do not export e-waste, which is worth
saying explicitly because a great deal of the industry does.
