## 2. Posting times by method

| Method | Time to post after initiation |
|---|---|
| PayPal | Immediate, usually within minutes |
| Visa / Mastercard / Amex | 5–7 banking days |
| Discover | 5–7 banking days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–7 banking days — posts to the underlying card |
| Klarna Pay-in-4 | 2–5 days on Klarna's own cycle |
| Shop Pay | 5–7 banking days |
| Shop Pay Installments | Up to 10 days |
| USDC | Same day, at the rate on the day of refund |
| Store credit | Immediate |

"Banking days" excludes weekends and bank holidays in your country. A refund
initiated on the Friday before a long weekend can easily take ten calendar days
to appear while still being entirely on schedule.
