## 3. European Union

EU prices are shown **VAT-inclusive** and ship **DDP**. The price you see is the
price you pay, with nothing collected at the door.

| Country | VAT rate |
|---|---|
| Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, France | 21%, 21%, 20%, 20% |
| Germany | 19% |
| Ireland, Denmark, Sweden | 23%, 25%, 25% |
| Italy, Spain, Portugal | 22%, 21%, 23% |

Because VAT rates differ by country while our EUR price is uniform, our margin
varies slightly by destination. We absorb that rather than showing 27 different
prices.
