## 4. Lithium batteries

The Lumen 400 Headlamp and Cache 10K Power Bank contain lithium-ion cells,
which are regulated as dangerous goods in air transport. Consequences:

- These items ship **ground-only** within the US where the destination is
  reachable by ground. Expedited air service is unavailable on orders
  containing them.
- They cannot be shipped to Australia or New Zealand at all, because our
  FedEx International Priority lane to those countries is air-only and our
  account is not certified for lithium dangerous goods on that route.
- Orders to Japan and Singapore containing these items ship separately from
  the rest of the order and may arrive several days apart.
- Airlines occasionally refuse a battery shipment at the gateway even when
  correctly declared. If that happens the parcel returns to Reno and we refund
  the item automatically.

If you place a mixed order containing a battery item and apparel, we split it.
You will see two tracking numbers and no additional charge.
