## 2. Why fabric softener ruins technical clothing

Fabric softener works by depositing a waxy cationic film on the fibres. On
cotton that feels soft. On merino it coats the fibre and destroys the moisture
management and odour resistance you paid for. On a DWR shell it fills the
surface texture the water repellency depends on, and the garment wets out
immediately.

Do not use it on anything in our range. If your washing machine has a softener
dispenser, check it is empty — residue from a previous load is enough to cause
the problem.

The same applies to **powder detergent**, which does not fully dissolve in cold
water and leaves abrasive residue in the membrane. Use liquid.
