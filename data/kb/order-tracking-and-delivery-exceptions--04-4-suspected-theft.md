## 4. Suspected theft

If the proof of delivery looks correct but the parcel is gone, it was most
likely taken. We do not require a police report to make you whole, and we will
not ask you to prove a negative.

For orders over $300 we may ask you to file a report so we can pursue the
carrier claim, but your refund or replacement is not contingent on it.
