## 5. Refused parcels

If you refuse a DDU parcel because of the duty bill, the carrier returns it to
the originating fulfilment centre. When it arrives and clears inspection we
refund the merchandise value.

We do not refund:

- the duty or brokerage fee — we never received that money, the carrier and
  the customs authority did
- the original outbound shipping charge, where one was paid
- the return freight, which is deducted at cost

Duties already paid on a DDU shipment are recoverable only from your own
customs authority, not from us. Most authorities have a formal reclaim process
for returned goods; we will supply the commercial invoice and proof of export
to support your claim. Email claims@everstorm.example and we will send the
paperwork within two business days.
