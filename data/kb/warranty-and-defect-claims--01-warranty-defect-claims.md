# Warranty & Defect Claims

Everstorm warrants every product against manufacturing defects for **12 months
from the date of delivery**. This is a commercial guarantee offered in addition
to your statutory rights, which it does not limit — see
`returns-addendum-eu-uk.md` §5 if you are in the EU or UK, where the legal
guarantee runs longer.
