## 6. Processing times from Rotterdam

Rotterdam runs longer resolution times than our US nodes, typically by 2–4
business days, driven by customs paperwork on inbound returns. We are working
on it. If your return is time-sensitive, say so in the portal notes and we will
prioritise the inspection.
