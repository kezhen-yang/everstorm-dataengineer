# Instalment Plans — Klarna & Shop Pay

Two providers, two markets, and a set of consequences that reach well beyond
the payment page.

## 1. Availability

| | Klarna Pay-in-4 | Shop Pay Installments |
|---|---|---|
| Markets | Selected EU countries | **US only** |
| Not available | UK, US, Canada, AU/NZ, Japan, Singapore | Everywhere except US |
| Schedule | 4 payments, every 2 weeks | 4 payments over 6 weeks |
| Interest | None | None |
| Credit check | Soft, no score impact | Soft, no score impact |
| Order minimum | €35 | $50 |
| Order maximum | €1,000 | $3,000 |

Klarna countries: Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland,
Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden.

**Klarna is not available in the UK**, despite Klarna operating there — our UK
entity is not enrolled with them. UK customers see Shop Pay and cards only.
