Section: 9. What we have not solved

## 9. What we have not solved

- Our shells are still made from petroleum-derived nylon, recycled or not.
- We have no closed-loop recycling for 3-layer laminates; the layers cannot
  currently be separated economically.
- Our Vietnam facilities are grid-powered and that grid is coal-heavy.
- We do not have Scope 3 emissions data we would be willing to publish.

Full reporting is at everstorm.example/responsibility.
