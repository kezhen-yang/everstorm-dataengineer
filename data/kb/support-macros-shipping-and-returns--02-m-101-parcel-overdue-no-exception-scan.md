## M-101 · Parcel overdue, no exception scan

> Thanks for flagging this, and sorry for the wait. I can see your parcel left
> our {FC} facility on {DATE} and the last scan was {LAST_SCAN}. Our target for
> {REGION} is {LO}–{HI} business days, so this is running behind.
>
> Scans can lag the parcel by up to 12 hours, so it may well be closer than the
> tracking suggests. I've opened a trace with {CARRIER} — that takes 3–8
> business days domestically.
>
> You don't need to wait for that to finish. Would you rather I send a
> replacement now, or refund you?

**Agent notes.** Offer the choice immediately. Do not make the customer wait for
a trace to conclude before we make them whole. Domestic trace threshold is 7
days without movement, international 14.
