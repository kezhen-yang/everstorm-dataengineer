## 9. Weather and force majeure

Extreme weather, natural disaster, industrial action and customs backlogs can
extend transit beyond the targets in §4. Everstorm is not liable for carrier
delays arising from these causes.

We will, however, refund any paid expedited surcharge if a delivery misses its
expedited commitment by more than 24 hours, including when the cause was
weather. Standard-service shipping is not refunded for weather delay, because
standard service carries a target rather than a commitment.
