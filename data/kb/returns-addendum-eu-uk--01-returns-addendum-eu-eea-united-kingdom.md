# Returns Addendum — EU, EEA & United Kingdom

Applies **only** to orders delivered to an address in the EU/EEA or the United
Kingdom. If your delivery address is in the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand,
Japan or Singapore, this document does not apply to you — see
`returns-addendum-us-canada.md` or the main policy.

This addendum sits on top of `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md`. Where the two
differ, whichever is **more favourable to you** applies.
