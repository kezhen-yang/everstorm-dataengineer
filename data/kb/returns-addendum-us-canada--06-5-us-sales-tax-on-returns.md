## 5. US sales tax on returns

Sales tax is refunded automatically and in full with the merchandise refund, at
the rate originally charged. You do not need to do anything.

Where a partial return crosses a threshold that changes shipping eligibility —
for example returning one item drops your basket under $75, where free shipping
applied — we do **not** retroactively charge you the shipping. The original
terms stand.
