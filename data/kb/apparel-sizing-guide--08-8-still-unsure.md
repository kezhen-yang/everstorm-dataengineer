## 8. Still unsure

Email sizecare@everstorm.example with your chest, waist and height, and what you
plan to wear underneath. We will give you a specific recommendation rather than
pointing you back at the chart.

You can also order two sizes and return the one that does not fit — the 30-day
window and free US returns exist precisely for this. Note that Final Sale and
custom-embroidered items cannot be returned, so get the size right before
ordering those. See `final-sale-and-custom-orders.md` §6.
