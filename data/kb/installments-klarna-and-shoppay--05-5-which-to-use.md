## 5. Which to use

If you are going to keep the item, either is fine and genuinely free.

If there is a realistic chance you will return or exchange it — a first-time
boot purchase, an unfamiliar size, a gift — pay by card or PayPal. The
instalment plans are worst exactly where our returns process is most used, and
that combination produces most of the frustration we see.
