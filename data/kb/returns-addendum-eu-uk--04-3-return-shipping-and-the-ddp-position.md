## 3. Return shipping and the DDP position

EU orders ship **DDP** — duties and import VAT were paid by us and included in
your price. Consequences on return:

- There is no duty for you to reclaim, because you never paid it.
- We reclaim the import VAT ourselves.
- Your refund is the full price you paid, less the return label cost.

**UK orders ship DDU.** You will have paid import VAT and a DPD brokerage fee at
delivery. Those are **not refundable by us** — reclaim them from HMRC using the
commercial invoice, which we will send on request. This is the single most
common point of confusion for UK customers, who reasonably assume the EU DDP
line covers them. It does not.
