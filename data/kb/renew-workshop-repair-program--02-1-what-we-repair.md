## 1. What we repair

| Category | Typical repairs | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Hardshell & softshell | Seam re-tape, zipper replacement, patch, re-proof | 2–3 weeks |
| Insulated outerwear | Baffle repair, down re-fill, shell patch | 3–4 weeks |
| Merino base layers | Darning, cuff and hem rebuild | 2 weeks |
| Trousers | Knee and seat reinforcement, zip, hem | 2 weeks |
| Footwear | Resole, heel counter rebuild, eyelet and lace hardware | 4–6 weeks |
| Packs | Buckle and webbing replacement, zip, frame sheet, hipbelt | 2–3 weeks |

We do not repair electronics. The Lumen 400 Headlamp and Cache 10K Power Bank
are replaced under warranty or recycled — see §7.
