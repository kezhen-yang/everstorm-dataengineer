# Returns Addendum — United States & Canada

Applies **only** to orders delivered to a US or Canadian address. Customers in
the EU, EEA or UK should read `returns-addendum-eu-uk.md` instead — the
statutory rights described there do not exist in US or Canadian law and nothing
in this document creates them.
