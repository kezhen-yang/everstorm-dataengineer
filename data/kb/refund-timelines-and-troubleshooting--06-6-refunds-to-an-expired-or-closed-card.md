## 6. Refunds to an expired or closed card

Card networks route a refund to the replacement card automatically where your
bank has issued one against the same account.

If the account is closed entirely, the refund bounces back to us, typically
after 7–10 days. We will contact you to arrange store credit or a bank
transfer. Do not wait silently for this — if your card is closed, tell us when
you start the return and we will set it up correctly from the beginning.
