## 2. Material sourcing

| Material | Standard | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| Merino wool | Mulesing-free, ZQ certified | Annual third-party audit |
| Down | Responsible Down Standard, traceable to farm | RDS chain of custody |
| Recycled nylon | Global Recycled Standard, 92% post-consumer | GRS |
| Recycled cotton | GRS, 60% post-consumer | GRS |
| Leather | Leather Working Group Gold tanneries | LWG |
| Aluminium hardware | Recycled content, no specific standard | Supplier declaration |

The last row is weaker than the others and we list it that way on purpose. We
take the supplier's word on hardware recycled content and have not audited it
independently.
