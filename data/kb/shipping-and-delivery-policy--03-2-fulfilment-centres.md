## 2. Fulfilment centres

We operate three fulfilment centres:

| Code | Location | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| RNO | Reno, Nevada | US West, and international lanes for Canada, AU/NZ, Japan and Singapore |
| HBG | Harrisburg, Pennsylvania | US Midwest and US East |
| RTM | Rotterdam, Netherlands | EU/EEA and the United Kingdom |

Orders route automatically to the closest node holding stock. If a single order
contains items split across two nodes, we ship it as two parcels at no extra
charge to you, and you will receive two tracking numbers. We do not hold the
first parcel back to consolidate.
