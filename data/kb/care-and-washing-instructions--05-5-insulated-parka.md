## 5. Insulated parka

Wash with a down-specific detergent — ordinary detergent strips the natural oils
from down and the fill never recovers loft.

Tumble dry on low with two or three clean dryer balls or tennis balls to break
up clumps. This takes **two to three hours** and you must not rush it. Down that
is put away damp will mildew and rot from the inside, and the parka is finished.

Pull it out every 30 minutes and break clumps by hand. When it is dry it will
feel noticeably lighter and lofty again.
