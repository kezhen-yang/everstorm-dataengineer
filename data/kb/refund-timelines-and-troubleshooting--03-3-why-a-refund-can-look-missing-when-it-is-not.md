## 3. Why a refund can look missing when it is not

**It posted to a different line than you expected.** Card refunds often appear
dated to the original transaction date rather than the refund date, so the
credit shows up several screens back in your statement rather than at the top.
Search by amount, not by date.

**Apple Pay and Google Pay refund to the underlying card.** If you have since
changed the default card in your wallet, the refund still goes to the card used
originally.

**It was an authorisation, not a charge.** Cancelled orders void the hold rather
than refunding it. Nothing was captured, so nothing is credited — see
`payment-methods-and-processing.md` §2.

**Your bank is holding it.** Some issuers batch incoming credits and post them
overnight or on a weekly cycle. We cannot influence this.
