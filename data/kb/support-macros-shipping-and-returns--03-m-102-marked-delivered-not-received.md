## M-102 · Marked delivered, not received

> I'm sorry — that's a frustrating one. Before anything else, two quick things
> that resolve this more often than you'd expect: give it 24 hours, and check
> with neighbours, any building office, and around the property.
>
> If it still hasn't turned up after that, reply here and I'll sort a
> replacement or refund straight away. I've already pulled the carrier's proof
> of delivery and I can see {POD_DETAIL}.

**Agent notes.** 24-hour wait once, never twice. Do not require a police report
— for orders over $300 you may ask for one to support the carrier claim, but the
refund is not contingent on it. Never imply the customer is lying.
