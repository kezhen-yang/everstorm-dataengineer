## 5. Getting embroidery right the first time

Because we cannot take it back, we try hard to stop mistakes before the needle
moves:

- You approve a rendered proof by email before production starts.
- Production does not begin until you reply. There is no timeout that starts it
  for you.
- You can cancel free of charge at any point before you approve the proof.
- After approval, cancellation is not possible — the item goes into the
  production queue same day.

If we embroider something different from what you approved, that is our error:
full refund or remake, your choice.
