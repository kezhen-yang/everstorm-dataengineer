## 3. Harmonised System codes we declare

We declare honestly and at full value. We will not mark a shipment as a gift,
understate the value, or split an order across parcels to slip under a de
minimis threshold. Requests to do so are declined — this is customs fraud and
we will not participate in it, regardless of how routine the request sounds.
