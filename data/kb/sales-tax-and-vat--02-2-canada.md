## 2. Canada

GST/HST and provincial sales tax apply at the destination rate:

| Province | Combined rate |
|---|---|
| Alberta, NWT, Nunavut, Yukon | 5% GST |
| British Columbia | 5% GST + 7% PST |
| Saskatchewan | 5% GST + 6% PST |
| Manitoba | 5% GST + 7% RST |
| Ontario | 13% HST |
| Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI, Newfoundland | 15% HST |
| Quebec | 5% GST + 9.975% QST |

Canadian orders ship **DDU**, so this tax is collected by the carrier at
delivery along with a brokerage fee, not by us at checkout. See
`international-shipping-and-customs.md`.
