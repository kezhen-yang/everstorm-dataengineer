## 3. "Delivered" but nothing arrived

This is the single most common exception we handle. The procedure:

1. **Wait 24 hours.** A meaningful share of parcels scanned delivered turn up
   the following day. Drivers scan at the end of a route, or scan the wrong
   stop, or a parcel gets left on a truck overnight.
2. **Check the obvious places.** Behind planters and gates, with neighbours,
   at a building concierge or mailroom, in a parcel locker, with a leasing
   office. Carriers leave parcels in genuinely creative places.
3. **Check the carrier's proof of delivery.** Most carriers record a GPS
   coordinate and sometimes a photo. If the photo is not your door, that is
   decisive evidence and speeds everything up.
4. **Email claims@everstorm.example** with the order number and what you found.

We ask for the 24-hour wait because it resolves a large fraction of cases at
zero cost to anyone. We do not ask you to wait twice.
