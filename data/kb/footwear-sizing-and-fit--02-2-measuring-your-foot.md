## 2. Measuring your foot

Do it at the end of the day — feet swell measurably, and a boot fitted in the
morning can be a size too small by evening.

1. Stand on a sheet of paper, heel against a wall, wearing your hiking sock.
2. Mark the tip of your longest toe. It is not always the big toe.
3. Measure wall to mark in centimetres.
4. Repeat for the other foot. **Fit the larger one** — most people have a
   meaningful difference.

|---|---|---|
| 25.0 | 7 | 8.5 |
| 25.7 | 8 | 9.5 |
| 26.4 | 9 | 10.5 |
| 27.1 | 10 | 11.5 |
| 27.8 | 11 | 12.5 |
