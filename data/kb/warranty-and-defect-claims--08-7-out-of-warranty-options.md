## 7. Out-of-warranty options

If your item is past 12 months, or the failure is wear rather than defect, you
are not out of options:

- **Renew Workshop** repairs most things for a modest fee — see
  `renew-workshop-repair-program.md`.
- **Lifetime Repair Credit** gives you store credit equal to the repair cost if
  you would rather buy new than repair.
- **Spare parts** — buckles, snaps, laces, cord locks — are free. Email
  parts@everstorm.example and we post them out.
