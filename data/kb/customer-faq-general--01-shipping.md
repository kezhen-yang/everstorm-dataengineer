# Customer FAQ — General

Short answers. Each links to the document that governs, and where the two ever
disagree, the policy document wins.

## Shipping

**How long will my order take?**
US West 2–3 business days, Midwest 3–4, East Coast 4–5, Canada and EU/UK 5–8,
Japan and Singapore 4–6, Australia and New Zealand 7–10. Plus processing:
orders before 14:00 local warehouse time ship the same day.
→ `shipping-and-delivery-policy.md`

**When is shipping free?**
US, Canada and EU standard shipping is free at $75 and over, calculated on
merchandise after discounts, before tax and shipping. No free-shipping threshold
on AU/NZ, Japan or Singapore.
→ `shipping-and-delivery-policy.md` §5

**Do you ship to PO boxes?**
No. Also no freight forwarders and no APO/FPO.

**Why did I get two tracking numbers?**
Your order split across two fulfilment centres, or it contained a lithium
battery item that must ship separately. No extra charge either way.

**Can I change my address after ordering?**
Within 30 minutes, usually yes. After the parcel is manifested, no — refuse
delivery and we will refund.
→ `shipping-and-delivery-policy.md` §7
