## Warranty and repairs

**How long is the warranty?**
12 months against manufacturing defects. EU and UK customers also have longer
statutory rights that we do not limit.

**What isn't covered?**
Wear: abrasion, pilling, fading, punctures, and DWR wearing off.

**Can you fix it if it's out of warranty?**
Yes. Renew Workshop repairs anything we have ever sold, at any age, for anyone.
Spare parts are free.
