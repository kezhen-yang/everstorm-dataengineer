## 5. Our fraud screening

Orders are scored automatically. A small number are held for manual review,
which adds up to one business day before dispatch.

Triggers include a mismatch between billing and shipping country, a freight
forwarder address, several cards tried on one order, or an unusually large first
order. Review is not an accusation, and most held orders release without us ever
contacting you.

If we cannot verify an order we cancel and refund rather than ship. You are
never charged for an order we decline to fulfil.
