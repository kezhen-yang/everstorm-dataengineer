# Renew Workshop Repair Program

Everstorm partners with **Renew Workshop**, an independent repair facility in
Bozeman, Montana, to keep gear in service rather than in landfill. Anything we
have ever sold can be repaired, regardless of age, whether you bought it new or
second-hand, and whether or not you are the original owner.
