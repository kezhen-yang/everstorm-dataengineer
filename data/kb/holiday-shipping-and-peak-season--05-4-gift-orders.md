## 4. Gift orders

- **Gift receipts** can be added at checkout and print without prices.
- **Gift returns** are refunded as store credit emailed to the recipient. The
  original purchaser is not notified, which is the point.
- **Gift messages** are printed on a card, not on the packing slip.
- We do not offer gift wrapping. Everything ships in the same recycled carton
  it normally would.

See `gift-cards-and-store-credit.md` for how the store credit itself works.
