## 3. StormCell-3L

- 3-layer laminate: face fabric, membrane, tricot backer
- **20,000 mm** hydrostatic head / **20,000 g/m²/24h** breathability (20k/20k)
- Fully seam-taped, PFAS-free membrane

Genuinely waterproof, not merely resistant. The 20k/20k rating means it will
hold out sustained rain and still pass moisture vapour out under moderate
exertion.

**Where it fails:** it wets out. When the DWR on the face fabric stops working,
the face saturates, and although no water passes the membrane, the garment feels
clammy and breathability collapses. People read this as the jacket leaking. It
is not leaking — it needs re-proofing. See
`care-and-washing-instructions.md` §4.

Delamination — the membrane separating from the face or backer — is a genuine
manufacturing defect and is covered. It looks like bubbling or lifting on the
inside surface.

**Used in:** Tempest 3L Shell Jacket, Drift Insulated Parka (shell).
