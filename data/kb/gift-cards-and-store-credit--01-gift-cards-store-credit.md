# Gift Cards & Store Credit

Two different instruments that people routinely confuse.

| | Gift card | Store credit |
|---|---|---|
| How you get it | You buy it | We issue it — return, goodwill, repair credit |
| Expires | Never | Never |
| Refundable to cash | No | No |
| Transferable | Yes | No, tied to the email it was issued to |
| Usable with discount codes | Yes | Yes |
| Counts toward free shipping threshold | Yes | Yes |
