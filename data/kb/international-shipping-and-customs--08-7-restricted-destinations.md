## 7. Restricted destinations

Beyond the OFAC list, a handful of destinations are excluded for practical
reasons — no reliable carrier lane, or an import regime that makes returns
impossible. These currently include Russia, Belarus, and any address in a
territory under active carrier embargo. Checkout will block these addresses.
