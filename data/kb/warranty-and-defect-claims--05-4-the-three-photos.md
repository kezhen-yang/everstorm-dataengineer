## 4. The three photos

Claims stall on bad photos more than on anything else. We need:

1. **The whole item**, laid flat, in daylight, so we can assess overall
   condition and confirm it is ours.
2. **The defect, close up and in focus**, filling the frame.
3. **The defect in context** — a mid-distance shot showing where on the item it
   sits.

For footwear, add a fourth photo of the outsole tread. Tread wear tells us how
many miles the boot has done, which is often the deciding factor between a
defect and end-of-life.
