## 9. Resoling

The Summit GTX is resolable through Renew Workshop for $110, typically twice
before the upper is beyond saving. The Creek Approach Shoe is not resolable —
the sticky rubber compound is bonded rather than stitched. See
`renew-workshop-repair-program.md`.
