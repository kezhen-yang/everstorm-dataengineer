## 8. Lost, stolen or damaged parcels

Summarised here; the full procedure is in
`order-tracking-and-delivery-exceptions.md`.

1. **Marked delivered but not received** — wait 24 hours, check with neighbours
   and any building office, then email claims@everstorm.example.
2. **No tracking movement** for 7 days domestic or 14 days international — we
   open a carrier trace.
3. **Visible transit damage** — photograph the box *before* unsealing it and
   attach the pictures to your claim email.
