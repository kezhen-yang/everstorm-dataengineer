## 2. The three consequences worth knowing before you choose one

**Refunds take longer.** Materially longer, and this is not something we can fix
from our side.

| Method | Time to post after we initiate |
|---|---|
| PayPal | Immediate |
| Card | 5–7 banking days |
| Klarna Pay-in-4 | 2–5 days on Klarna's own cycle |
| Shop Pay Installments | Up to 10 days |

Our support data shows instalment tickets staying open roughly twice as long as
card tickets, almost entirely because of this. If you expect to return the item,
consider paying by card or PayPal.

**Instant Exchange is unavailable.** Both providers refuse the $1
re-authorisation the mechanism depends on. You can still exchange — we hold the
replacement in reserve and ship once your return is inspected, which adds
roughly a week. See `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md` §4.

**Partial returns get complicated.** Both providers recalculate the remaining
instalments rather than issuing a clean refund, so what you see is a revised
schedule rather than money coming back. If you have already paid more than the
adjusted total, the difference is refunded to your card, on top of the timelines
above.
