## 3. Where to manage the plan

With the provider, not with us. We can see that a plan exists and what the
order total is; we cannot see your schedule, change a payment date, or take a
payment on their behalf.

- **Klarna** — klarna.com or the Klarna app. They email the schedule after
  order confirmation. If it did not arrive, log in rather than asking us to
  resend, because we never had it.
- **Shop Pay** — shop.app or the Shop app, under Installments.
