## 4. Klarna Pay-in-4

Available in Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland,
Italy, the Netherlands, Spain and Sweden. **Not available in the UK** despite
Klarna operating there — our UK entity is not enrolled.

Four equal payments, the first at checkout and the rest every two weeks. No
interest. Klarna runs a soft credit check that does not affect your score.

Klarna emails your payment schedule shortly after order confirmation. If it
does not arrive, log in at klarna.com or use their app — we cannot resend it,
because we never had it.

**Refunds on Klarna take longer than any other method we offer**, typically 2–5
days after we initiate, because Klarna reschedules your remaining instalments on
their own processing cycle rather than ours. Once we have initiated the refund
the delay is entirely on Klarna's side and we cannot accelerate it. We flag this
at checkout, and it is the most common refund complaint we receive.
