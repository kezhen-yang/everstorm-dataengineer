## 6. Softshell and Trail-Stretch

Machine cold, gentle, liquid detergent. Hang dry or tumble low.

**Never iron Trail-Stretch and never use a hot dryer.** The 8% spandex degrades
at temperatures well below what feels safe for nylon, and once the stretch is
gone the garment is permanently baggy at the knees and seat. This is the most
common avoidable damage we see on returns.
