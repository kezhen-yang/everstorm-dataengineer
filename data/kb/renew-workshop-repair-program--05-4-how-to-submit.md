## 4. How to submit

1. Open a ticket at everstorm.example/renew.
2. Describe the damage and upload two photos.
3. We email a prepaid label within one business day.
4. Renew Workshop assesses on arrival and emails a quote.
5. Approve the quote, decline it, or take the Lifetime Repair Credit.
6. Repaired items ship back free.

If you decline the quote and do not want the credit, we return the item to you
free of charge. There is no assessment fee and never has been.
