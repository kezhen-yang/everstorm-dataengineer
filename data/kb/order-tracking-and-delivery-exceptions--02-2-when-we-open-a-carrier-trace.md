## 2. When we open a carrier trace

We open a trace without needing you to ask when:

- **Domestic (US):** no tracking movement for 7 consecutive days
- **International:** no tracking movement for 14 consecutive days

A trace asks the carrier to physically look for the parcel at the last-scanned
facility. It takes 3–8 business days for domestic and up to 15 for
international. You do not have to wait for the trace to conclude before we make
you whole — see §6.
