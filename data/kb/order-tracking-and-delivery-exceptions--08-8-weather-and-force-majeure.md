## 8. Weather and force majeure

Everstorm is not liable for delays caused by weather, natural disaster,
industrial action or customs backlog. We will refund a paid expedited surcharge
where the expedited commitment is missed by more than 24 hours, including for
weather. Standard service carries a target, not a commitment, and is not
refunded on that basis.

During a declared carrier service suspension, traces are not available and the
clocks in §2 pause. We will tell you when that applies to your parcel rather
than letting you chase it.
