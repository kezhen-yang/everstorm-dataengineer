## 4. Missed payments

Klarna charges a late fee capped by local regulation and pauses your ability to
use Pay-in-4 until you catch up. Shop Pay charges no late fee but reports
extended delinquency to credit bureaus.

Neither affects your Everstorm account. We do not cancel orders, withhold
shipments, or restrict future purchases because of a dispute with an instalment
provider. Your relationship with them is separate from your relationship with
us.
