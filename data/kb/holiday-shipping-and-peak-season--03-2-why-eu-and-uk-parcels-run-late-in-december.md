## 2. Why EU and UK parcels run late in December

This is our worst-performing lane in peak season, consistently, and it is worth
understanding why.

Our EU and UK volume moves through a DHL Express hub in Leipzig. From late
November to early January that hub runs at capacity and DHL **formally suspends
its service guarantees** for the period. The published 5–8 business day target
for EU and UK becomes, in practice, 8–16 business days through December.

Because the guarantee is suspended at the carrier's end, no service refund is
available to us — which means we cannot pass one on to you for a *standard*
shipment. We can and do refund expedited surcharges under §9 of the shipping
policy where the expedited commitment is missed, and we apply that generously
during peak.

If you are ordering to the EU or UK for a fixed date in December, order early
and use expedited. This is the honest recommendation, not an upsell.
