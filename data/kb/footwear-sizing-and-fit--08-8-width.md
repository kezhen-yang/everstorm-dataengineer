## 8. Width

We build one width, roughly a US D for men and a US B for women. We do not offer
wide or narrow fittings, and saying so plainly is more useful than pretending
half a size up is the same thing.

If you normally need a 2E or wider, the Summit GTX will probably not work for
you. Email sizecare@everstorm.example before ordering and we will give you a
straight answer rather than a sale.
