## 5. Trousers

| Size | Waist | Hip | Inseam (regular) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | 28–29 | 35–36 | 32 |
| 30 | 30–31 | 37–38 | 32 |
| 32 | 32–33 | 39–40 | 32 |
| 34 | 34–35 | 41–42 | 32 |
| 36 | 36–37 | 43–44 | 32 |
| 38 | 38–39 | 45–46 | 32 |

Inseam options: **Short 30"**, **Regular 32"**, **Long 34"**. Not every size is
stocked in every inseam; the product page shows live availability.

The Switchback Trail-Stretch Pant has 8% spandex and accommodates roughly an
inch either side of the stated waist. The Granite Softshell Pant has no stretch
panel at the waistband and runs true with no give — measure carefully for that
one.
