## 5. Shipping rates

All rates in USD unless marked.

| Basket total | USA Standard | USA Expedited | Canada Standard | EU Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under $75 | $7.95 | live rate at checkout | $14.95 | €9.95 |
| $75 and over | **Free** | live rate at checkout | **Free** | **Free** |

The $75 threshold is calculated on merchandise value **after** any discount
codes are applied and **before** tax and shipping. A $80 basket with a 10%
code falls to $72 and no longer qualifies.

Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Singapore are quoted at live carrier rates at
checkout; there is no free-shipping threshold on those lanes.

EU standard shipments are sent **DDP** — duties and import VAT are paid by us
and included in the price you see. Every other international destination ships
**DDU**, meaning the carrier will collect duties and any brokerage fee from you
before or at delivery. See `international-shipping-and-customs.md` for detail.
