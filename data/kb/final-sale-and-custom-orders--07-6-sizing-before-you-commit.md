## 6. Sizing before you commit

For any non-returnable item we strongly recommend ordering a returnable stock
item in the same size first, confirming the fit, and then placing the custom
order. Customer support will happily talk you through
`apparel-sizing-guide.md` or `footwear-sizing-and-fit.md` before you buy —
email sizecare@everstorm.example.

We would rather spend ten minutes on a size question than process a complaint
about a $400 jacket that cannot go back.
