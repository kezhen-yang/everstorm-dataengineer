## 2. Signing in

We support passkeys and TOTP two-factor authentication. We deliberately do not
offer SMS two-factor — SIM-swap attacks make it weaker than the alternatives.

Password resets go to your registered email and expire after 30 minutes. If the
link has expired, request a new one rather than contacting support; we cannot
extend an expired link.
