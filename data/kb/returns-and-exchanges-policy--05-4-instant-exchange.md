## 4. Instant Exchange

For size exchanges we can ship the replacement before we have your original
back.

We place a temporary **$1 authorisation** on your card and release the new size
as soon as the carrier scans the return label. The authorisation drops off when
our returns team receives the original item.

If the original never arrives within 21 days of the label scan, we charge the
full price of the replacement to the card on file. We email twice before that
happens.

Instant Exchange is unavailable on Klarna and Shop Pay Installments orders,
because those providers do not support a re-authorisation of this kind.
