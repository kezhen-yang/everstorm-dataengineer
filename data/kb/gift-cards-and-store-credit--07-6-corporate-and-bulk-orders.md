## 6. Corporate and bulk orders

For 10 or more gift cards, email billing@everstorm.example rather than buying
them one at a time. We issue them as a single batch with a consolidated invoice,
optional custom branding on the delivery email, and staggered delivery dates.

Bulk gift cards are non-refundable once issued, so confirm quantities before we
generate the codes.
