## 4. United Kingdom

UK orders ship **DDU**. Import VAT at 20% plus a DPD brokerage fee is collected
at delivery.

The UK is not covered by the EU DDP arrangement in §3. This is the most common
tax question we get, and the answer is consistently disappointing to UK
customers who have read the EU section first.
