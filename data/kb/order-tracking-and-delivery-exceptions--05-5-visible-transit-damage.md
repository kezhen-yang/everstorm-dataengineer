## 5. Visible transit damage

**Photograph the box before you open it.** This is the one step that materially
changes the outcome, because a carrier damage claim is nearly unwinnable
without a picture of the sealed carton.

Then:

1. Photograph the damaged contents alongside the box.
2. Keep all packaging until the claim closes — carriers occasionally ask to
   inspect it.
3. Email the photos to claims@everstorm.example.

We file the carrier claim ourselves. You should never have to negotiate with a
carrier on our behalf; if an agent asks you to, that is a training failure and
we would like to know about it.
