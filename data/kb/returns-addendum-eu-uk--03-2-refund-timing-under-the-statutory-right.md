## 2. Refund timing under the statutory right

Where you exercise the statutory right, we must refund within **14 days** of
either receiving the goods back or receiving proof you have sent them,
whichever is earlier. This is faster than our standard inspection-then-refund
sequence, and we honour it.

Under our ordinary 30-day policy the sequence in §6 of the main policy applies:
receipt, inspection within 3 business days, then initiation.
