## 3. Outerwear

Chest is given as a body range the garment is designed to fit, not a flat
measurement of the garment itself. Sleeve is measured from centre-back.

| Jacket size | Chest (body) | Sleeve from centre back |
|---|---|---|
| S | 37–39 | 33 |
| M | 39–42 | 34 |
| L | 42–45 | 35 |
| XL | 45–48 | 36 |

Applies to the Tempest 3L Shell, Drift Insulated Parka and Kestrel Windshell.

The Tempest 3L is cut with deliberate room for layering and will feel large over
a T-shirt. That is intentional; it is a shell. The Kestrel Windshell is cut
trim and is the one garment in the range where sizing up is usually the right
call.

The Drift Insulated Parka runs about half a size large because of the baffle
loft. If you are between sizes on the Drift, take the smaller.
