## 4. Re-proofing a DWR shell

When water stops beading on the face fabric, the DWR needs renewing. Do this
before you conclude the jacket is leaking — a wetted-out shell feels exactly
like a leaking one from the inside.

1. Wash the garment first. DWR will not bond to a dirty face fabric. Use a
   technical cleaner, not ordinary detergent.
2. Apply a spray-on DWR to the **outer** surface while the garment is damp.
   Spray-on is better than wash-in for 3-layer shells because it does not treat
   the inner tricot.
3. Wipe any excess from seams and zips.
4. Heat-set: tumble dry on low for 20 minutes, or press with a warm iron
   **through a towel**, never directly.

Heat is what activates the finish. Skipping step 4 is why most home re-proofing
does not work.

Expect to do this every 10 washes or so. See `fabric-and-materials-glossary.md`
§4.
