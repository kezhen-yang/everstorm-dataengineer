Section: Escalation thresholds

## Escalation thresholds

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Refund over $500 | Team lead approval |
| Goodwill credit over $75 | Team lead approval |
| Return 30–45 days late, first request | Agent discretion, approve |
| Return over 45 days late | Team lead |
| Warranty claim, Summit GTX sole separation | **Auto-approve, no review** |
| Any mention of legal action or a regulator | Escalate immediately, do not respond on substance |
| Customer alleges fraud on their card | Route to billing within 1 hour |
