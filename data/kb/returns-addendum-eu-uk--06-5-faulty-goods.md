## 5. Faulty goods

Statutory rights on faulty goods are separate from, and additional to, any
return window. In the EU you have a minimum **two-year** legal guarantee of
conformity. In the UK you have up to six years to bring a claim under the
Consumer Rights Act 2015, though the burden of proof shifts to you after six
months.

Our 12-month warranty in `warranty-and-defect-claims.md` is a commercial
guarantee offered **in addition to** these rights, not a replacement for them.
Nothing in our policy limits your statutory rights, and an agent telling you
"it's out of warranty" at 14 months has given you an incomplete answer where
the fault is a conformity defect.
