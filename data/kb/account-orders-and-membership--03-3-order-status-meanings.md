## 3. Order status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Payment authorised, not yet picked |
| In review | Held by fraud screening, up to 1 business day |
| Picking | Being assembled at the fulfilment centre |
| Manifested | Handed to the carrier — no longer modifiable |
| Partially shipped | One parcel of a split order has left |
| Delivered | Carrier delivery scan recorded |
| Cancelled | Not fulfilled, authorisation voided |

Modification is possible only at Pending. Once an order reaches Picking we
cannot change the address, the size, or the contents. See
`shipping-and-delivery-policy.md` §7 for the 30-minute address window.
