## 1. What is Final Sale

An item is Final Sale when it is:

- discounted more than 50% off original retail
- an end-of-life colourway being cleared
- a returned item resold through the Second Summit programme
- a sample or pre-production unit

Final Sale is marked in three places before you buy: on the product listing, in
the cart line item, and on the checkout summary. If it was not marked in all
three, it is not Final Sale and we will take the return.
