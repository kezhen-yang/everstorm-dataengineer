# Order Tracking & Delivery Exceptions

What to do when a parcel does not behave. This is the operational companion to
§8 of `shipping-and-delivery-policy.md`.

## 1. Reading a tracking page

| Status | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| Label created | We have printed a label. The parcel may still be in the building. |
| Manifested | The carrier has the parcel on their manifest. We can no longer reroute it. |
| Departed facility | It left a sort hub. The next scan may be 12–36 hours away. |
| In transit | Between scans. Long gaps are normal on international lanes. |
| Out for delivery | On a vehicle, scheduled for today. |
| Delivered | A driver pressed a button. This is not proof of receipt — see §4. |
| Exception | Something went wrong. Read the sub-status; it is usually address-related. |

Scans can lag reality by up to 12 hours. A parcel that "hasn't moved since
yesterday" has very often moved.
