## 5. Order history and invoices

Three years of order history in your account. Invoices download as PDF.

To add a company name or VAT number to an invoice, do it before dispatch. After
dispatch we can reissue with corrected company details but cannot retroactively
remove VAT that was correctly charged. See `sales-tax-and-vat.md` §7.
