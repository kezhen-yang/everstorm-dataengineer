## 1. Where we ship

We deliver to the United States, Canada, the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom,
Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Singapore.

We cannot ship to:

- PO boxes of any kind, in any country
- Freight forwarders or parcel-consolidation addresses
- Any destination on the current OFAC sanctions list
- APO/FPO military addresses (this is a carrier limitation, not a policy choice)

If an order is placed to an address in one of these categories, our address
validation usually catches it at checkout. If it slips through, we cancel the
order and refund in full within one business day rather than attempt delivery.
