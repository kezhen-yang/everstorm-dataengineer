## 8. Shipping emissions

Shipping is our largest operational emission source, and expedited air freight
is many times worse per parcel than ground.

- Standard shipping is the default, and free over $75.
- The Rotterdam node exists partly to keep EU orders off transatlantic air.
- We do not offer carbon offsets at checkout. The evidence for offset quality is
  poor and we would rather not sell you an absolution we do not believe in.

If you do not need something quickly, choose standard. It is the most useful
thing a customer can do on this front and it also costs you less.
