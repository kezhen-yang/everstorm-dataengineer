## 7. End of life

When something genuinely cannot be repaired, send it to us anyway with a
recycling ticket. We sort it into:

- **Merino** — reprocessed into insulation batting
- **Synthetic shell fabric** — mechanically recycled into industrial felt
- **Footwear** — ground for track and playground surfacing
- **Electronics and lithium cells** — certified e-waste processing, never
  landfill and never shipped overseas

Recycling labels are free. See `sustainability-and-materials-sourcing.md` for
what actually happens to the material.
