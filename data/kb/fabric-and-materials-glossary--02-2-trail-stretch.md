## 2. Trail-Stretch™

- 92% recycled nylon, 8% spandex
- DWR coated, PFAS-free since the 2025 season
- 4-way stretch, roughly 25% elongation

A softshell face fabric for movement. Wind-resistant but not windproof, water
repellent but not waterproof. Dries fast and takes abrasion far better than
merino.

**Where it fails:** the DWR wears off, and the spandex degrades with heat. Never
tumble dry Trail-Stretch on anything above low, and never iron it — the spandex
melts well below the temperature that feels safe for nylon.

**Used in:** Switchback Trail-Stretch Pant, Kestrel Windshell.
