## 6. State-level consumer protections

A few states impose requirements on return policies. Where any of these conflict
with our stated policy, the state rule applies to residents of that state:

- **California** — return policies must be conspicuously posted; ours is, at
  everstorm.example/returns.
- **New York** — retailers must post the policy and honour it as written.
- **Florida, Virginia, Ohio** — require disclosure where no refunds are
  offered; not applicable to us, since we do offer refunds.

None of these extends your return window beyond 30 days.
