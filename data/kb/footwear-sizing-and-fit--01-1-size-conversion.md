# Footwear Sizing & Fit

Covers the Summit GTX Hiking Boot (EVS-FTW-001) and the Creek Approach Shoe
(EVS-FTW-002).

## 1. Size conversion

| US Men | US Women | EU | UK |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 8.5 | 40 | 6 |
| 8 | 9.5 | 41 | 7 |
| 9 | 10.5 | 42 | 8 |
| 10 | 11.5 | 43 | 9 |
| 11 | 12.5 | 44.5 | 10 |

Half sizes are available throughout in US Men and US Women. EU sizing moves in
whole sizes except at the top of the range.

Note the relationship: **US Women = US Men + 1.5**. A US Men's 9 and a US
Women's 10.5 are the same physical shoe. We list both because customers ask in
both.
