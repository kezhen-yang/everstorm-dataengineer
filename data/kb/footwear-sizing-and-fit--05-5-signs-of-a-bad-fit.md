## 5. Signs of a bad fit

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Toes hit the front on descent | Too short — size up |
| Heel lifts when walking uphill | Too long, or heel too wide — try a half size down or a heel lock lacing |
| Numbness across the top of the foot | Laced too tight, or too low volume |
| Pinching at the widest part of the foot | Too narrow — size up half |
| Hot spots at the ball of the foot | Insole compressed or wrong shape |

Almost all of these are fixable by changing size, and none of them get better
with wear. A boot that hurts in the shop will hurt on the trail.
