## M-106 · Refund not received

> The refund was initiated on {DATE}. Posting time depends on the provider —
> {METHOD} typically takes {DAYS}.
>
> Two things worth checking: card refunds often appear dated to the *original*
> transaction, so search your statement by amount rather than date. And Apple
> Pay and Google Pay refund to the underlying card, not the wallet.
>
> If it hasn't landed by {DATE+10}, come back to me and I'll send you the ARN so
> your bank can trace it.

**Agent notes.** Sequence is receipt → inspection within 3 business days →
initiation → provider posting. Do **not** say "refunds are issued the same day
your return is scanned" — that phrasing is from an outdated FAQ and it is wrong.
See `refund-timelines-and-troubleshooting.md` §1.
