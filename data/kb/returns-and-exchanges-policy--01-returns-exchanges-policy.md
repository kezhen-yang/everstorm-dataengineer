# Returns & Exchanges Policy

**Everstorm Outfitters — Document ROX-2025-05, effective 18 May 2025**

This revision extended the return window from 21 days to 30 and introduced the
Instant Exchange hold. Regional variations are in
`returns-addendum-eu-uk.md` and `returns-addendum-us-canada.md`; where an
addendum grants you more than this document does, the addendum wins.
