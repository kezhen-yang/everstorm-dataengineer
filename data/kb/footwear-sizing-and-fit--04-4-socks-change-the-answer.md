## 4. Socks change the answer

Fit the boot with the sock you will actually hike in. A midweight merino hiking
sock takes up roughly a third of a size compared to a thin liner sock.

If you fit with a thin sock and hike with a thick one, the boot will be too
tight. If you do the reverse, your heel will lift and you will get blisters.
