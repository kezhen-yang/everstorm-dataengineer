## 9. Repairs rather than replacement

If something has failed rather than worn out, check
`warranty-and-defect-claims.md` first. If it is genuinely worn, Renew Workshop
will usually fix it for less than you would expect — see
`renew-workshop-repair-program.md`.

Wash the item before sending it in. Renew returns unwashed items unrepaired.
