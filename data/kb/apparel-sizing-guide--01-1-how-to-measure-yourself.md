# Apparel Sizing Guide

All measurements in inches unless stated. Everstorm apparel is unisex unless a
product explicitly says otherwise.

## 1. How to measure yourself

You need a soft tape and preferably a second person.

| Measurement | How |
|---|---|
| Chest | Around the fullest part, under the arms, tape level, arms relaxed at your sides |
| Waist | At your natural waist, the narrowest point, not at your beltline |
| Hip | Around the fullest part of your seat |
| Sleeve | From the centre-back of your neck, over the shoulder and down a slightly bent arm to the wrist bone |
| Inseam | From crotch seam to the floor, in the shoes you will wear |

Measure over the base layer you actually intend to wear. For outerwear, measure
over the midlayer too — a shell that fits over a T-shirt and not over a fleece
is the single most common sizing complaint we receive.
