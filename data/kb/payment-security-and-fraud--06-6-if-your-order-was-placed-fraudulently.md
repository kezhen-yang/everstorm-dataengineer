## 6. If your order was placed fraudulently

Email billing@everstorm.example immediately with the order number from the
confirmation email.

If it has not shipped, we cancel and void the authorisation. If it has shipped,
we recall the parcel where the carrier allows and refund on receipt, or refund
in full and absorb the loss where they do not.

Then contact your bank and report the card compromised — the fraudulent order at
our store is rarely the only one.
