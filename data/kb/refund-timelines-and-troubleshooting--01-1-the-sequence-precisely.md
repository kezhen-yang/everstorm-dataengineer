# Refund Timelines & Troubleshooting

## 1. The sequence, precisely

A refund passes through four stages. Confusing them is the cause of almost every
"where is my refund" ticket.

| Stage | Who controls it | How long |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Carrier delivers your return to the warehouse | Carrier | 3–10 days in transit |
| 2. Warehouse receipt and inspection | Us | Within 3 business days |
| 3. Refund initiated to your provider | Us | Same day as inspection clears |
| 4. Credit posts to your account | Your provider | See table below |

We control stages 2 and 3, which together take up to 3 business days. Stage 4 is
entirely your payment provider and is the part that varies.

> **Correcting an old inconsistency.** Earlier versions of our payments FAQ said
> "we issue refunds the same day your return is scanned." That was loose
> wording: the *scan* is stage 1, and the refund is issued after inspection at
> stage 3. The sequence above is accurate and matches
> `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md` §6. Where an older FAQ copy disagrees, this
> document and the returns policy govern.
