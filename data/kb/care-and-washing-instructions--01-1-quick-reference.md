# Care & Washing Instructions

## 1. Quick reference

| Item type | Wash | Dry | Iron | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Merino base layer | Cold, gentle, wool-safe detergent | Flat dry | Low | Avoid fabric softener |
| DWR shell | Machine cold, no powder soap | Tumble low 10 min, then hang | No | Re-spray DWR every 10 washes |
| Insulated parka | Machine cold, down-specific wash | Tumble low with dryer balls | No | Takes 2–3 hours to dry fully |
| Softshell / Trail-Stretch | Machine cold, gentle | Hang or tumble low | **Never** | Spandex melts |
| Canvas pack | Hand-wipe, mild soap | Air | n/a | Remove frame sheet first; never machine wash |
| Leather boots | Brush dirt dry, then damp cloth | Air, away from heat | n/a | Condition every 3 months |
