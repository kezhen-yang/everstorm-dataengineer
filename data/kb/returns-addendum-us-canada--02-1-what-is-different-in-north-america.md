## 1. What is different in North America

| | United States | Canada |
|---|---|---|
| Return window | 30 days from delivery | 30 days from delivery |
| Return label | Free, prepaid | Prepaid, $15 deducted from refund |
| Duties on return | n/a | Not refundable by us — see §4 |
| In-person returns | Not available | Not available |
| Statutory withdrawal right | None | None |

There is no US or Canadian equivalent of the EU right of withdrawal. Our 30-day
policy is a commercial promise we make voluntarily, and it is the whole of what
you are entitled to.
