# Sales Tax & VAT

## 1. United States

We calculate and collect sales tax automatically in **42 states plus the
District of Columbia**, wherever we have economic nexus. Rates are applied at
the destination address, down to local district level.

Five states have no statewide sales tax and we collect nothing there:

| State | Note |
|---|---|
| Alaska | Some municipalities levy local tax, which we do collect |
| Delaware | None |
| Montana | None — our home state |
| New Hampshire | None |
| Oregon | None |

Apparel is fully or partially exempt in several states, and our tax engine
applies the exemption automatically:

| State | Apparel treatment |
|---|---|
| Minnesota | Clothing exempt; footwear exempt |
| New Jersey | Clothing exempt; most footwear exempt |
| Pennsylvania | Clothing exempt; sporting footwear **taxable** |
| Vermont | Clothing exempt |
| Massachusetts | Exempt up to $175 per item, taxed above |
| New York | Exempt under $110 per item statewide; local tax may still apply |
| Rhode Island | Exempt up to $250 per item |

So a $245 Summit GTX boot is taxable in Pennsylvania as sporting footwear, and
a $89 merino crew is not taxable in New York but a $389 shell jacket is taxable
on the portion above $110. The engine handles this; the detail is here because
customers do ask why two items in one basket were taxed differently.
