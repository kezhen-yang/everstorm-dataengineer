## 5. Wash before you send

Renew Workshop will return unwashed items unrepaired. This is not fussiness —
technicians work with their hands on fabric all day.

Follow `care-and-washing-instructions.md` for the item type. If the item is too
damaged to wash safely, say so in the ticket and send it anyway; they will
handle it.
