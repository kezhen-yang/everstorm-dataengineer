## 3. Fit guidance by model

**Summit GTX Hiking Boot** — a stiff backpacking boot on a medium-volume last.
It runs **true to size in length** but is cut narrow through the forefoot. If
you have a wide foot, size up half a size rather than hoping it stretches; the
GTX membrane limits how much the upper can give.

Allow about a thumb's width between your longest toe and the front of the boot
when standing. On a steep descent your foot slides forward, and without that
gap you will lose toenails. This is the single most common fitting error we see.

**Creek Approach Shoe** — a sticky-rubber approach shoe on a low-volume last.
Runs **half a size small**. Most people take a half size up from their street
shoe. Cut narrow through the heel, which suits narrow feet well and wide heels
poorly.
