## 1. Your statutory right of withdrawal

EU consumers have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within
**14 days** of receiving the goods, under the Consumer Rights Directive
(2011/83/EU). UK consumers have an equivalent 14-day right under the Consumer
Contracts Regulations 2013.

**Everstorm's own 30-day window is longer and more generous, so in almost every
case you should simply use it.** The statutory right matters in two situations
where it gives you something our policy does not:

1. **Final Sale and custom items.** Our policy excludes these. The statutory
   right generally does not, except for goods made to your personal
   specification. A Final Sale jacket in a stock size remains withdrawable
   under statute; a jacket embroidered with your name does not.
2. **Return shipping cost.** Under the statutory right you may return goods at
   your own cost and we must refund the **standard outbound** shipping you
   paid. Under our policy the label is prepaid but we deduct its cost from
   your refund on international lanes.

To exercise the statutory right specifically, email returns@everstorm.example
with the words "right of withdrawal" and your order number. We will confirm in
writing within one business day.
