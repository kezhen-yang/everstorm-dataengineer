## 4. Repair before replacement

The single largest environmental lever in apparel is keeping a garment in use
longer. Renew Workshop exists for that reason rather than as a marketing line.

Every product we have ever sold is repairable, regardless of age, purchase
channel, or whether you are the original owner. Spare parts are free.

In the 2025 financial year Renew Workshop completed 4,180 repairs. We do not
know how many of those displaced a new purchase, and we are not going to
estimate a number we cannot substantiate.
