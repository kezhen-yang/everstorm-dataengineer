## 6. Carrier pickup blackouts

No carrier collects on these dates, so nothing dispatches:

| Date | Affected |
|---|---|
| 25–26 December | All nodes |
| 1 January | All nodes |
| 27 December (Rotterdam only) | EU and UK orders |
| 4 July, 25 November | US nodes (Reno, Harrisburg) |

Orders placed on a blackout date ship the next working day.
