## 6. Tax on refunds

Refunded in full and automatically with the merchandise, at the rate originally
charged. Partial returns refund tax proportionally. See
`refund-timelines-and-troubleshooting.md` §5.

Import duty and VAT on DDU shipments are **not** refundable by us, because we
never collected them. Reclaim from your own authority — the process and the
paperwork we will provide are in `international-shipping-and-customs.md` §5.
