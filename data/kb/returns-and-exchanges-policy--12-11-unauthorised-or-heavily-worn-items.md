## 11. Unauthorised or heavily worn items

Items showing heavy wear (mud, pet hair, smoke) are either shipped back to you
or donated to charity at our discretion, minus a **$15 handling fee**.

We email you before doing either, and we hold the item for 14 days waiting for
a reply. We would rather return something to you than bin it.
