## 5. Second Summit

Returned items that cannot be sold as new are graded and resold at 40–60% off
rather than destroyed. See `final-sale-and-custom-orders.md` §7.

We do not destroy unsold inventory. Anything that cannot be sold through Second
Summit goes to gear libraries, outdoor education programmes, or material
recycling.
