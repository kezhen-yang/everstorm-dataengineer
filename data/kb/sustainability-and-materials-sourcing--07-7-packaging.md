## 7. Packaging

- Cartons: 100% recycled content, recyclable
- Polybags: eliminated across the range as of 2025
- Hangtags: recycled card, soy ink
- Tape: paper, water-activated

We do not offer gift wrapping. Everything ships in the same carton it otherwise
would.
