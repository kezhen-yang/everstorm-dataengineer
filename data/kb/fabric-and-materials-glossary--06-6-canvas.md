## 6. Canvas

A 12 oz waxed cotton-blend used on the Haul 45 Canvas Pack. Abrasion resistant,
repairable, and it ages well.

**Where it fails:** it absorbs water when the wax wears thin, and it is heavy
compared to nylon. Re-wax annually with any standard cotton wax. Never machine
wash canvas — it strips the wax and the pack is never the same afterwards.
