# Sustainability & Materials Sourcing

What we actually do, with the uncomfortable parts included.

## 1. PFAS

All Everstorm DWR finishes and membranes have been PFAS-free since the 2025
season. No exceptions remain in the current range.

The honest trade-off: PFAS-free DWR is less durable than the fluorocarbon
chemistry it replaced. It needs re-proofing more often — roughly every 10 washes
rather than every 20. We think that is the right call and we would rather tell
you than let you conclude the jacket is faulty. See
`care-and-washing-instructions.md` §4.
