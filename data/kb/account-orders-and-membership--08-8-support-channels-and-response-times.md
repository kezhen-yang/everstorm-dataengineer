## 8. Support channels and response times

| Channel | Hours | Target response |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat | 08:00–18:00 MT, Mon–Fri | Immediate |
| Email | 24/7 intake | 24 hours (48 during peak) |
| Phone | 08:00–18:00 MT, Mon–Fri | Under 10 minutes, except peak |
| Instagram DM | Business hours | 48 hours |

During peak season (1 December – 5 January) chat extends to 07:00–20:00 MT and
email becomes the fastest channel, which is the reverse of the rest of the year.
See `holiday-shipping-and-peak-season.md` §7.

Summit members skip the queue on chat and phone.
