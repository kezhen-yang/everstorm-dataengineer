## M-107 · International return, duty question

> Happy to set the return up. Two things specific to international returns:
> the prepaid label costs {LABEL_COST} and comes out of the refund, and the
> duties you paid aren't refundable by us — we never received that money.
>
> You can reclaim those from {CUSTOMS_AUTHORITY}. I'll send the commercial
> invoice and proof of export so you have what they'll ask for.

**Agent notes.** EU customers paid no duty — those orders are DDP. **UK
customers did**, because the UK ships DDU. Do not tell a UK customer their
order was duties-paid; this is our most frequent factual error and it generates
a complaint every time.
