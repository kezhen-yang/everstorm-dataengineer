## 3. What you still get

Non-returnable does **not** mean unsupported. On a Final Sale or custom item you
still have:

| | Available |
|---|---|
| Change-of-mind return | No |
| Size exchange | No |
| 12-month warranty on manufacturing defects | **Yes** |
| Wrong item shipped | **Yes** — full refund or replacement |
| Damaged in transit | **Yes** — full refund or replacement |
| Renew Workshop repairs | **Yes** |
| Lifetime Repair Credit | **Yes** |
| Free spare parts | **Yes** |

The exclusion is about changing your mind. It is not a waiver of your rights
when the product is faulty, and any agent who tells you "it's Final Sale, there
is nothing we can do" about a seam failure has given you the wrong answer.
