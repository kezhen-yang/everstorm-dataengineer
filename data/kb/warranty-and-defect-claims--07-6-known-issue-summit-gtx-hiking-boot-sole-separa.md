## 6. Known issue — Summit GTX Hiking Boot sole separation

We have an elevated rate of outsole separation on the **Summit GTX Hiking Boot
(EVS-FTW-001)**, concentrated in units from production lots manufactured before
March 2026. The failure presents as the outsole lifting from the midsole across
the forefoot, often on both boots within a few weeks of each other.

If you have this failure:

- It is approved automatically. You do not need to argue the case.
- The 12-month window is **extended to 24 months** for this specific failure
  mode on this specific product.
- Replacements ship from stock built after the lot change.
- If you would rather have a refund than another pair, we will do that instead,
  at full original price, outside the normal return window.

This is a manufacturing fault and we are treating it as one.
