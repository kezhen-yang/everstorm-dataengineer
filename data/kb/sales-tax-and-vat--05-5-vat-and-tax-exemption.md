## 5. VAT and tax exemption

**EU businesses** with a valid VAT number can have VAT removed under the reverse
charge. Enter the number in the VAT field at checkout; we validate against VIES
in real time. It must be registered for intra-community supply — a domestic-only
registration will validate but not qualify.

**US resellers and tax-exempt organisations** should email
billing@everstorm.example with the exemption certificate before ordering. We
cannot apply an exemption retroactively; the order has to be placed against an
already-approved exempt account.
