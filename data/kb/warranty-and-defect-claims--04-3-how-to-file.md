## 3. How to file

1. Open the returns portal at everstorm.example/returns.
2. Enter your order number and email.
3. Choose **Warranty Claim** rather than Refund or Exchange.
4. Upload **three photos**, described in §4.
5. Describe what happened and roughly how much use the item has had. Be honest
   about this — it helps rather than hurts, and it is the fastest route to an
   approval.

If you no longer have the order number, warranty@everstorm.example can find it
from your email address.
