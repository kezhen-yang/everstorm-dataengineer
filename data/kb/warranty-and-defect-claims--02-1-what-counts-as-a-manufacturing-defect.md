## 1. What counts as a manufacturing defect

A defect is a failure of materials or construction under normal use. The
clearest examples, and the ones we approve without argument:

- **Seam failure** — stitching that pulls apart along a seam line
- **Sole separation** — the outsole or midsole delaminating from the upper
- **Zipper failure** — teeth shedding, slider splitting, box failing at the base
- **Hardware failure** — buckles shearing, bartacks tearing out, snaps pulling
  through the fabric
- **Delamination** — a membrane separating from its face fabric or backer
- **Waterproofing failure** on a StormCell-3L garment inside 12 months, where
  the garment has been washed according to `care-and-washing-instructions.md`
