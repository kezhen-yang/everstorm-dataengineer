## 2. Eligibility checklist

- Unworn, unwashed, no odours, tags attached
- Original shoe box included, placed **inside** an outer carton — a shoe box
  used as the shipping box arrives destroyed and fails inspection
- Electronics (power banks, headlamps) unopened, unless the item is faulty
- No pet hair, smoke, mud or trail wear

"Tried on indoors" is fine and always has been. We expect you to try things on.
Boots walked on gravel are not returnable as unworn, because we cannot resell
them.
