## 4. DWR (Durable Water Repellent)

A surface finish, not a fabric. It makes water bead and roll off.

DWR is a **consumable**. It wears off through abrasion and washing, on every
garment from every manufacturer. Ours is PFAS-free across the range as of the
2025 season, which is better environmentally and slightly less durable than the
fluorocarbon chemistry it replaced. We think that is the right trade and we
would rather say so than hide it.

Re-proof every 10 washes or whenever water stops beading. Wash-in and spray-on
products both work; spray-on is better for a 3-layer shell because it does not
treat the inner surface.

Failed DWR is **not a warranty defect** unless it fails inside a handful of
washes. See `warranty-and-defect-claims.md` §2.
