## M-105 · Return eligibility, outside window

> I've had a look and that order was delivered {N} days ago, which is outside
> our 30-day window, so I can't process it as a standard return.
>
> A few things worth checking before we leave it there:
> - If you bought between 1 Nov and 31 Dec, the window runs to 31 January.
> - If you're a Ridge or Summit member, you have 60 days rather than 30.
> - If the problem is a fault rather than a change of mind, that's a warranty
>   matter and covered for 12 months.

**Agent notes.** Work through all three before declining. Agents have discretion
to approve up to 45 days on a first request with no prior returns — use it
rather than escalating.
