## 6. Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the shipping label is created. Label creation
happens before the parcel physically leaves the building, so there is normally
a gap between the email and the first real scan.

Status updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after the parcel is scanned at
the origin terminal. A tracking page that says "Label created" for half a day
is not a problem. One that says it for four days is — see
`order-tracking-and-delivery-exceptions.md`.
