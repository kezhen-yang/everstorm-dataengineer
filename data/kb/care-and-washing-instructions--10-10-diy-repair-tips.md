## 10. DIY repair tips

- **Seam separation up to 3 cm** — clean the area and apply Gear-Aid SeamGrip,
  cure 24 hours.
- **Missing jacket snap** — email parts@everstorm.example for a free snap kit.
- **Broken boot lace** — 63 inches of paracord and a surgeon's knot will get you
  home.
- **Small shell puncture** — a Tenacious Tape patch on the outside, rounded
  corners so it does not peel.
