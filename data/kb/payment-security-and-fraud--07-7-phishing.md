## 7. Phishing

We will never email you asking for card details, your password, or a
verification code. Legitimate Everstorm email comes only from
`@everstorm.example`. Look closely at the domain: lookalike domains are the
standard trick.

Our order emails never contain a payment link. If an email claims your order is
held pending payment and asks you to click through and pay, it is not from us —
forward it to security@everstorm.example and delete it.
