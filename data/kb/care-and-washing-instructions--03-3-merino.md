## 3. Merino

Cold, gentle cycle, wool-safe liquid detergent. Turn inside out to reduce
pilling at friction points.

Dry flat. Merino stretches under its own weight when wet, and a garment hung wet
on a hanger will come out longer than it went in, permanently.

Never tumble dry merino. It felts — the fibres interlock irreversibly — and the
garment shrinks a full size or more. This is not recoverable and it is not a
warranty matter.

You do not need to wash merino often. That is the point of it. Airing overnight
handles most trips.
