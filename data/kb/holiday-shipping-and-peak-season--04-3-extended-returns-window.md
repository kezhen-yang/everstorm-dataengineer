## 3. Extended returns window

Any purchase made between **1 November and 31 December** has its return window
extended to **31 January** of the following year, regardless of the 30-day rule
that normally applies.

This exists so gifts bought in November are still returnable after they are
opened in late December. It is automatic — you do not need to ask for it, and
the returns portal applies it for you.

Items marked Final Sale and custom-embroidered items remain non-returnable
during this window, unless defective.
