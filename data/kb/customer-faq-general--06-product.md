## Product

**How do I pick a size?**
Measure your chest and trust the number over the letter. Tees are athletic-cut.
The Drift Parka runs half large, the Kestrel Windshell runs trim, the Creek
Approach Shoe runs half small.
→ `apparel-sizing-guide.md`, `footwear-sizing-and-fit.md`

**My jacket is leaking.**
Probably not. When the DWR wears off, the face fabric saturates and the jacket
feels clammy even though the membrane is intact. Re-proof it first.
→ `care-and-washing-instructions.md` §4

**Can I put it in the washing machine?**
Merino yes, cold and gentle, wool detergent, dry flat and never tumble. Shells
yes, cold, no powder, no softener. Canvas packs never.

**My boots are falling apart.**
If it is outsole separation on a Summit GTX, it is auto-approved under warranty
and covered for 24 months rather than 12.
→ `warranty-and-defect-claims.md` §6
