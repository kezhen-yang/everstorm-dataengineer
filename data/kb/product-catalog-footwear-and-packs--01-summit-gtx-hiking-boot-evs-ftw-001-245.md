## Summit GTX Hiking Boot — EVS-FTW-001 — $245

A stiff, supportive backpacking boot for multi-day loads on rough ground.

| | |
|---|---|
| Sizes | US Men 7–11, US Women 8.5–12.5, half sizes throughout |
| Weight | 1,290 g per pair (US Men 9) |
| Upper | Full-grain nubuck leather with GORE-TEX membrane |

True to size in length, narrow through the forefoot. Wide feet should size up

typically twice.
