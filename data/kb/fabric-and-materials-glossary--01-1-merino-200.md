# Fabric & Materials Glossary

What our materials are, what they do, and where they fail.

## 1. Merino 200

- 200 g/m² weight, **18.9 micron** fibre
- 100% merino wool, mulesing-free, sourced from certified New Zealand growers
- Machine washable cold on a wool cycle

The micron number is the one that matters for comfort: 18.9 is fine enough that
almost nobody finds it itchy. Coarse wool starts around 23 microns.

Merino regulates temperature in both directions, insulates when damp, and
resists odour for days because the fibre structure does not host the bacteria
that cause it. That last property is why it is worth the price on a multi-day
trip.

**Where it fails:** abrasion. Merino is weak against rock and pack straps
compared to synthetics, and 200 g/m² is a base layer weight, not an outer layer.
Pilling at friction points is normal and is not a defect. Holes at the elbows
after a season of heavy use are wear, not a warranty matter — though Renew
Workshop will darn them for about $25.

**Used in:** Ridgeline Merino 200 Crew, Ridgeline Merino 200 Bottom, Stormglass
Merino Beanie.
