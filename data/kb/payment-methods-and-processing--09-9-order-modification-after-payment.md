## 9. Order modification after payment

We cannot add items to an existing order, change sizes, or apply a discount code
retroactively. Place a second order and return the first, or cancel within the
30-minute window and start again.

Discount codes cannot be applied after the fact under any circumstances. This is
a hard limit in the payment platform, not a policy we are choosing to enforce.
