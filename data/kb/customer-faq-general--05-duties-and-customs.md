## Duties and customs

**Will I be charged duty?**
EU orders ship DDP — nothing to pay. **Everywhere else, including the UK**,
ships DDU and the carrier collects duty plus a brokerage fee at delivery.
→ `international-shipping-and-customs.md`

**Can you mark it as a gift or lower the value?**
No. That is customs fraud and we will not do it.

**Can I get my duty back on a return?**
Not from us — we never received it. Claim from your own customs authority; we
will supply the paperwork.
