## 3. Canadian return labels

Prepaid via Canada Post, with **$15 deducted from your refund** to cover the
cross-border lane. Canadian returns route to Reno, NV.

The deduction is waived entirely if the return is our fault: wrong item
shipped, defective on arrival, or damaged in transit. Select the matching
reason in the portal and the deduction disappears automatically. If it does not,
email us and we will credit it back — do not let a $15 line item go
unchallenged because the portal misfired.
