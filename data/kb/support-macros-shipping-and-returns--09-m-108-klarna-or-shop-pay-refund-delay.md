## M-108 · Klarna or Shop Pay refund delay

**Agent notes.** Never promise a date you do not control. Klarna 2–5 days, Shop
Pay Installments up to 10. These are our slowest methods and the ticket will
stay open — set the expectation early rather than letting it become three
chases.
