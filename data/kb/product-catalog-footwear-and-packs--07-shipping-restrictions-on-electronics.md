## Shipping restrictions on electronics

Both the Lumen 400 and the Cache 10K contain lithium-ion cells and are
regulated as dangerous goods in air transport. Consequences:

- Ground-only within the US; no expedited air service on orders containing them
- **Cannot ship to Australia or New Zealand at all**
- Ship separately from the rest of a mixed order, at no extra charge, and may
  arrive several days apart

Full detail in `international-shipping-and-customs.md` §4.

Electronics must be **unopened** to be returned under the standard 30-day
window, unless faulty. They are not repairable through Renew Workshop — they are
replaced under warranty or recycled through certified e-waste processing.
