## 6. Cryptocurrency

We accept **USDC on Ethereum Mainnet** for orders over $500, through Coinbase
Commerce. The exchange rate locks for 15 minutes at checkout. Send the exact
amount to the address shown; an underpayment holds the order until you top it
up.

We do not accept any other token or chain. Sending USDC on a different chain, or
a different token to that address, will in most cases lose the funds
permanently and we cannot recover them.

Crypto refunds are issued in USDC at the **rate on the day of refund**, not the
rate you paid. If USDC has moved against you in between, that difference is
yours. Most customers prefer store credit for this reason, and we offer it as an
alternative on every crypto refund.
