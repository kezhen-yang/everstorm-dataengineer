## 5. What happens next

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 1 business day |
| Assessment decision | 3 business days from complete photo set |
| Replacement dispatch on approval | Next business day |
| Requests for more information | Pause the clock until you reply |

Approved claims ship a replacement in the same size and colour where available.
If the exact item is discontinued, we offer the nearest current equivalent or a
full-value store credit — your choice.

You are not asked to return the defective item in most cases. Where we do want
it back, usually because we are investigating a pattern, we send a prepaid
label and the replacement ships immediately rather than waiting.
