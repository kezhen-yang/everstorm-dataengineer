## 3. Lifetime Repair Credit

This is the part worth understanding, because it is unusual.

Submit a repair ticket and get a quote. If you decide you would rather buy new
than repair, **we give you store credit equal to the quoted repair cost** and
you keep the old item to do with as you like.

An example: your Tempest 3L needs a $70 zipper replacement. You can have the
repair for $70, or you can decline the repair, receive $70 in store credit
toward a new jacket, and keep the old one for wet chores.

The credit is capped at 40% of the item's current retail price, does not expire,
stacks with sales, and is not transferable. One credit per item per 24 months.
