## 8. Leather boots

**After every trip:** knock off the dirt, let them dry naturally, and brush
them. Mud left on leather draws moisture out of it.

**Drying:** room temperature, away from any heat source, stuffed with newspaper
to absorb moisture and hold shape. A radiator or a campfire will crack an upper
in one night, and cracked leather cannot be repaired.

**Conditioning:** every three months of regular use, or whenever the leather
looks dry or pale. Apply sparingly and let it absorb overnight. Over-conditioned
leather goes soft and loses the support the boot depends on, so less is more.

**GTX membrane:** do not apply wax-heavy or silicone treatments to a GTX boot.
They block the membrane and the boot stops breathing. Use a conditioner
specified as membrane-safe.
