## 5. Shipping rates

| Basket total | USA Standard | USA Expedited | Canada Standard | EU Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under $99 | $9.95 | live rate at checkout | $19.95 | €14.95 |
| $99 and over | **Free** | live rate at checkout | $9.95 | €7.95 |

Note three differences from the current policy, all of which favour the
customer today:

- The free-shipping threshold was **$99**, not $75.
- Free shipping applied to **US orders only**. Canada and the EU paid a reduced
  rate above the threshold rather than nothing.
- **All** international destinations shipped DDU under this revision, including
  the EU. EU orders moved to DDP with Revision 4.0.
