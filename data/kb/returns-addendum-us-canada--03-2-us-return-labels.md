## 2. US return labels

Free, prepaid, UPS. Drop at any UPS Store, Access Point or staffed counter, or
schedule a pickup through UPS at no cost.

Returns route to whichever node is closer to you:

- **West of the Rockies** → Reno, NV
- **East of the Rockies** → Harrisburg, PA

The label we generate already has the right destination on it. Do not reuse a
label from a previous order — it will send your parcel to the wrong coast and
add a week.
