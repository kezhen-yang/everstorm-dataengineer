## 6. Refund timeline

Warehouse receipt → inspection within **3 business days** → refund initiates.

Once initiated, posting time depends entirely on your payment provider:

| Method | Typical time to post |
|---|---|
| Visa / Mastercard / Amex (via Stripe) | 5–7 banking days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–7 banking days |
| PayPal | Immediate |
| Klarna Pay-in-4 | 2–5 days, on Klarna's own reschedule cycle |
| Shop Pay Installments | Up to 10 days |

> **Note on a known inconsistency.** Our payments FAQ has historically said
> "we issue refunds the same day your return is scanned." That describes the
> *scan*, not the *inspection*. The accurate sequence is the one above: receipt,
> then inspection within 3 business days, then initiation. The FAQ wording is
> being corrected. Where the two disagree, this document governs.
