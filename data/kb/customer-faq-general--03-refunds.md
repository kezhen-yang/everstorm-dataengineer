## Refunds

**How long do refunds take?**
Receipt → inspection within 3 business days → initiation. Then your provider:
PayPal immediate, cards 5–7 banking days, Klarna 2–5 days, Shop Pay Installments
up to 10.
→ `refund-timelines-and-troubleshooting.md`

**I don't see my refund.**
Search your statement by amount, not date — card refunds often post dated to the
original transaction. Apple Pay and Google Pay refund to the underlying card. If
it has been over 10 banking days, email billing@everstorm.example for the ARN.

**Do I get the shipping cost back?**
Only if you return the whole order. Partial returns keep the shipping charge.
