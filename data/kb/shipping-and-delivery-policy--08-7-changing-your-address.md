## 7. Changing your address

Contact logistics@everstorm.example within **30 minutes** of placing the order.
Within that window we can usually catch it before the pick wave.

Once the parcel is "Manifested" to the carrier we cannot reroute it, and
neither can the carrier at our request. Your options at that point are to
refuse delivery, which returns the parcel to the originating fulfilment centre
for a full refund, or to accept it and use the ordinary returns process.
