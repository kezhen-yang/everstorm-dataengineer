## 4. When to escalate to us

Contact billing@everstorm.example if:

- It has been more than 10 banking days since we told you the refund was
  initiated
- The amount is wrong
- You received a refund for an order you did not return
- Klarna or Shop Pay has not adjusted your instalments after 10 days

Include the order number. We will send you the **ARN (Acquirer Reference
Number)** for card refunds, which is the reference your bank needs to trace a
credit on their side. Most banks resolve a "missing" refund within a day once
they have the ARN.
