## 7. Currency

All prices display in USD except in the EU, where they display in EUR. Your card
issuer converts at their own rate and may add a foreign transaction fee we
neither set nor receive.

The EUR price is not a live conversion of the USD price. It is set independently
and includes the import duty and VAT we pay on DDP shipments — see
`international-shipping-and-customs.md` §1. This is why the EUR price is not
what you get by converting the USD price yourself.
