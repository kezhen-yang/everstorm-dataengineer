## 7. Exchanging for a different size

We expect fit to take a couple of attempts and the process is built for it.

| Current size | Return label cost | Replacement ships | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too small | Free | +0.5 US size | Width unchanged |
| Too large | Free | −0.5 US size | EU size per conversion chart |

Try boots on indoors, on carpet, with your hiking socks. Indoor try-on does not
affect returnability. Once there is grit in the outsole they are worn and
cannot be returned — see `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md` §2.

Instant Exchange means we ship the new size as soon as the carrier scans your
return label, so you are not waiting on the round trip. Not available on Klarna
or Shop Pay Installments orders.
