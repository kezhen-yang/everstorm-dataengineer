## 5. Shop Pay Installments

US only, four payments over six weeks, no interest, no credit impact.

On a partial refund, Shop Pay recalculates the remaining instalments downward.
On a full refund it voids the remaining payments and refunds anything already
taken. Posting can take up to 10 days — the longest of any method.

Shop Pay Installments orders are **not eligible for Instant Exchange**, because
the provider does not support the $1 re-authorisation that mechanism needs. Use
a standard exchange instead; see `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md` §4.
