## 2. Typical duty and tax rates

These are indicative only. Rates change, thresholds move, and your customs
authority has the final say. We cannot quote you a binding figure in advance.

| Destination | De minimis (approx.) | Typical total on a $200 order |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | CAD 20 (gifts CAD 60) | CAD 30–55 including brokerage |
| United Kingdom | £135 for duty; VAT from £0 | £40–55 |
| Australia | AUD 1,000 | AUD 20–30 GST |
| New Zealand | NZD 1,000 | NZD 30 GST |
| Japan | JPY 10,000 | JPY 2,000–4,000 |
| Singapore | SGD 400 | SGD 18–30 GST |

Apparel and footwear generally attract a higher duty rate than accessories or
electronics. Wool garments in particular can be dutiable at a higher line than
synthetics in several jurisdictions.
