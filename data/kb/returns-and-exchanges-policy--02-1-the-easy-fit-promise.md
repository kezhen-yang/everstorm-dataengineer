## 1. The Easy-Fit Promise

If your gear doesn't fit, or just isn't your vibe, send it back within **30
days of delivery** for a refund or a free size exchange.

The clock starts at the carrier's delivery scan, not at the order date and not
at the dispatch date. You can see the exact date the clock started on the
returns portal.
