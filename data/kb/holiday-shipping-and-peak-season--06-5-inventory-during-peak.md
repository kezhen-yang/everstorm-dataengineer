## 5. Inventory during peak

Stock moves fast and we oversell occasionally despite our best efforts. If we
cannot fulfil an item:

- You are emailed within one business day.
- The item is refunded immediately; we do not hold your money against a
  restock.
- The rest of the order ships as normal.
- Where a close substitute exists, we offer it with a discount code rather than
  substituting silently.
