## Returns

**How long do I have?**
30 days from delivery. Purchases from 1 Nov to 31 Dec run to 31 January. Ridge
and Summit members get 60 days.
→ `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md`

**Does it cost anything?**
Free in the US. Canada deducts $15. International labels cost $15–$25, deducted
from the refund.

**Can I return worn items?**
Tried on indoors, yes — always. Worn outdoors, no. Boots with grit in the
outsole cannot be resold.

**What can't be returned?**
Final Sale, custom embroidery, and opened electronics — unless faulty, in which
case all three are covered.
→ `final-sale-and-custom-orders.md`

**How do exchanges work?**
Instant Exchange ships the new size as soon as the carrier scans your return
label, against a $1 temporary hold. Not available on Klarna or Shop Pay
Installments.
