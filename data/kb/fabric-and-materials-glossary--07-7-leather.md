## 7. Leather

Full-grain nubuck on the Summit GTX upper. Conditioned at the factory, and it
needs conditioning roughly every 3 months of regular use.

**Where it fails:** drying out. Leather that dries cracks, and cracked leather
cannot be repaired, only patched. Never dry boots near a heat source — a
radiator or campfire will destroy an upper in a single night. Air dry only,
away from direct heat, stuffed with newspaper.
