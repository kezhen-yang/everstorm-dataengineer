## 1. Gift cards

Digital only, delivered by email, in denominations from $25 to $500. We do not
sell physical gift cards.

Delivery is usually within a few minutes. You can schedule delivery for a future
date and add a message at checkout. If the recipient never received it, check
their spam folder first, then email billing@everstorm.example — we can resend to
a corrected address, and the original code is deactivated in the process.

Gift cards:

- never expire, and carry no dormancy or maintenance fee
- can be combined, up to five per order
- can be used alongside a discount code
- can pay for part of an order, with the balance on any accepted method
- cannot be refunded to cash, except where state law requires it below a small
  residual balance

Gift-card balances are checked at everstorm.example/giftcard.
