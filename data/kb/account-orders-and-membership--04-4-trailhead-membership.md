## 4. Trailhead membership

Free. Join at checkout or in account settings.

| Tier | Annual spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Base | $0 | 1 point per $1, free returns, early sale access |
| Ridge | $500 | 1.25 points per $1, free expedited shipping, extended 60-day returns |
| Summit | $1,500 | 1.5 points per $1, free expedited, 60-day returns, free embroidery, priority support queue |

100 points = $5 in store credit. Points post when an order is delivered, not
when it ships, and expire after 24 months of account inactivity.

**The 60-day return window for Ridge and Summit members is a genuine extension
of the standard 30-day policy** in `returns-and-exchanges-policy.md`. It does
not extend to Final Sale or custom items, which remain non-returnable at every
tier.

Tier is assessed on a rolling 12-month spend and reviewed monthly. Returns
reduce your qualifying spend.
