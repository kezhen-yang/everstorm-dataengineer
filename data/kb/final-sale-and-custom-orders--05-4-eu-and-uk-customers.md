## 4. EU and UK customers

Your statutory right of withdrawal may still apply to Final Sale goods even
though our policy excludes them. Goods made to your personal specification —
anything embroidered with your name, or altered to your measurements — are
genuinely excluded from the statutory right. A discounted stock-size jacket is
not.

See `returns-addendum-eu-uk.md` §1. If you are in the EU or UK and want to
withdraw from a Final Sale purchase, email returns@everstorm.example with
"right of withdrawal" in the subject line and we will process it.
