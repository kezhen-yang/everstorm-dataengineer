Section: 7. Instant Exchange availability

## 7. Instant Exchange availability

Available on all US and Canadian orders paid by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or
PayPal.

**Not available** on Shop Pay Installments orders, because the provider does not
support the $1 re-authorisation the mechanism depends on. Shop Pay Installments
customers should use a standard exchange, where we hold the replacement in
reserve and ship once the return is inspected.
