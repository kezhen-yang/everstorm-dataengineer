## 6. What we do about it

Our decision tree, in the order we try things:

| Situation | Our action |
|---|---|
| Item in stock, you still want it | Replacement ships from the nearest node on expedited service, at our cost |
| Item out of stock in your size | Full refund to the original payment method |
| You no longer need it (trip passed) | Full refund, and we absorb the carrier loss |
| Trace recovers the parcel after we replaced it | Keep both, or use the prepaid label — your choice, no follow-up from us |

We settle first and pursue the carrier afterwards. Whether the carrier
ultimately pays us is our problem, not yours.
