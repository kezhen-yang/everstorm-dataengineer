## 7. Second Summit resale items

Returned items that fail resale-as-new inspection but remain perfectly
serviceable are sold through Second Summit at 40–60% off, graded:

| Grade | Condition |
|---|---|
| A | Try-on only, no visible wear, original tags absent |
| B | Light cosmetic marks, fully functional |
| C | Visible wear or a completed repair, disclosed in the listing |

All grades are Final Sale, and all carry the full 12-month defect warranty from
the date you receive them — not from the original purchase date.
