## 7. Invoices

Every order receives a VAT-compliant invoice by email. To add a company name and
VAT number, do it before dispatch — reply to the confirmation email or contact
billing@everstorm.example.

After dispatch we can reissue an invoice with corrected company details, but we
cannot retroactively remove VAT that was correctly charged at the time.
