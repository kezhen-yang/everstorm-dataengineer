## 7. Deleting your account

Email support@everstorm.example. We delete within 30 days.

What we must retain, and why:

- Order and transaction records, for tax and accounting law — typically 7 years
- Warranty claim records for the duration of any active warranty
- Anything subject to an open dispute or chargeback

Retained records are pseudonymised where we can do so without breaking the legal
obligation. We will confirm in writing what was deleted and what was kept.
