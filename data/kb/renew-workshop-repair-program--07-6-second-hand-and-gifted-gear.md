## 6. Second-hand and gifted gear

The programme applies to any Everstorm product regardless of where it came
from. You do not need a receipt, an order number, or to be the original
purchaser.

Warranty claims are different and do require proof of purchase — see
`warranty-and-defect-claims.md`. A repair is a service you pay for; a warranty
claim is a defect we pay for.
