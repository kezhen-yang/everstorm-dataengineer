## 2. When you are charged

We authorise at checkout and **capture when the parcel ships**. On a
multi-parcel order we capture each parcel separately as it dispatches, so you
may see two smaller charges rather than one.

An authorisation is a hold, not a charge. If an order is cancelled before
dispatch the authorisation is voided and the hold disappears from your account
within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank. No money ever moved, which is
why you will not see a matching refund line — there is nothing to refund.

This confuses people regularly. A voided authorisation vanishes; it does not
appear as a credit.
