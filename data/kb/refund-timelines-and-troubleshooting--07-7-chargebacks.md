## 7. Chargebacks

Please talk to us before filing one. A chargeback locks the transaction for the
duration of the dispute — typically 45–90 days — and during that window we are
barred by the card networks from issuing a refund even if we agree with you
entirely. Filing a chargeback on a refund that was already in flight is the
slowest possible route to your money.

We do not blacklist customers for filing chargebacks. We would just rather solve
it in three days than ninety.
