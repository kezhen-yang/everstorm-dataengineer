# Accounts, Orders & Trailhead Membership

## 1. Guest checkout versus an account

You can check out as a guest. An account gets you order history, saved
addresses, faster returns, and Trailhead points.

If you checked out as a guest and later create an account with the same email,
past orders attach automatically. You do not need to ask us to merge them.
