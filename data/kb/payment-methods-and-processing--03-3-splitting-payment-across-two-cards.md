## 3. Splitting payment across two cards

Not supported at checkout. The workaround: buy a digital gift card with the
first card, then apply the gift card at checkout and pay the balance with the
second. Gift cards are delivered by email within a few minutes — see
`gift-cards-and-store-credit.md`.
