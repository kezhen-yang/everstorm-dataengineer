## 2. What does not count

Wear is not a defect, and the distinction is the whole substance of a warranty
claim.

| Not covered | Why |
|---|---|
| Abrasion from rock, scree or pack straps | Wear |
| Pilling on merino | Normal characteristic of the fibre |
| DWR no longer beading | Consumable finish — re-proof it, see below |
| Punctures, tears from crampons, cuts | Damage, not defect |
| Fading from UV or repeated washing | Wear |
| Odour | Wear and laundering |
| Damage from a repair someone else attempted | Voids the claim on that component |
| Lost items | Not a warranty matter |

**DWR is the most common misunderstanding.** Durable Water Repellent is a
finish, not part of the fabric. It wears off. A jacket whose DWR has stopped
beading after 30 washes is behaving normally and needs re-proofing, not
replacing. A jacket whose DWR failed after four washes may well be a coating
defect and we will look at it — `care-and-washing-instructions.md` covers
re-proofing either way.
