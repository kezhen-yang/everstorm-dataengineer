## 7. Support hours during peak

Live chat extends to 07:00–20:00 MT from 1 December to 5 January. Email
response targets move from 24 hours to 48 hours; phone wait times routinely
exceed 20 minutes in the week before Christmas.

Email is genuinely the fastest channel during peak, which is the opposite of
the rest of the year.
